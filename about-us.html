About Us

Corporate Profile

Print that supports your reputation

Metro Graphics helps organisations turn ideas, information, and brand messages into well-made printed materials. Our team brings together file preparation, printing, finishing, and delivery support so clients can manage the complete job with one dependable partner.

One coordinated production team

Our Kandivali East facility supports pre-press, printing, binding, packaging, and other finishing requirements. Equipment and experienced operators work together to keep production practical, controlled, and consistent across short and large runs.

Quality checks at every important stage

We review artwork before production, monitor colour and registration during printing, and inspect the finished work before packing. Preventive maintenance and process checks help us deliver reliable results without unnecessary rework.

A broad range of print applications

Our work includes business publications, manuals, books, calendars, packaging, tags, stationery, and other specialised print requirements. We support focused quantities and larger production schedules while keeping communication clear and deadlines visible.

Metro Graphics supports publishers, businesses, institutions, and brands that need dependable print production for local and wider distribution.

 

From the

Founders Desk

Printing has always helped people share knowledge, stories, and ideas. Today, that purpose continues through thoughtful design, dependable production, and responsible delivery.

Technology continues to change the way printed communication is prepared and produced. We keep learning and improving so our work remains useful, efficient, and consistent.

Metro Graphics is shaped by hands-on printing experience and a practical understanding of modern production. Our people combine process knowledge with careful attention to the requirements of each job.

Along the way what has remained constant is our dedication to quality, our passion for excellence, our ability to stay ahead of the technology curve and our relentless adherence to deadlines when it comes to customer service.

Over the years we have been pioneers when it came to adopting technology:

  •  Our production setup includes modern offset equipment supported by in-house pre-press and finishing capability.
  •  We were also one of the early birds to introduce colour management and GMG software
  •  We were also early converts to leveraging the power of Digital pre-press to enhance the quality of our output within short deadlines.
  •  We are one of the few mid sized printing units that are fully integrated and handle all process from receiving artworks online to pre-press, CTP plate making, printing and post printing processes including binding etc., in-house.

Our team supports short and large runs for books, business publications, periodicals, and other specialised print work. Pre-press, plate preparation, printing, and finishing are coordinated to keep jobs organised from file receipt through completion.

Our customers include several prestigious publishers in the B2B domain as well as leading brands and MNc’s. We work with publishers, businesses, and brands that value clear communication, consistent production, and dependable delivery support.

We are well equipped to handle international jobs and have logistics partners who facilitate global door-to-door deliveries.

So if World class print quality, consistency in adhering to deadlines and timely deliveries is what you seek, Drop in a line …

We’d love to hear from you.
