Print Services

Pre-Press — every file made production-ready

Before anything reaches the press, our pre-press team checks the files — colour settings, images, fonts, and layouts. Catching issues here prevents costly errors later and keeps production on schedule.

  • Artwork review by specialists:
    Our specialists review your artwork in its native design software, apply only the corrections you approve, and prepare files that print exactly as intended.
  • In-house CTP platemaking:
    Our CTCP plate maker produces plates in-house, giving us direct control over plate quality — and cuts days off the production timeline.
  • Plate checks that control dot gain:
    We combine precision measuring instruments with press fingerprinting to monitor and fine-tune dot gain and other key variables before the run begins.

Press & Printing — controlled production, sheet after sheet

Offset and digital work alike run on planned settings, clear job specifications, and in-process checks. Colour, registration, paper handling, and the schedule are monitored throughout, so the approved result is the result you receive. Offset printing covers books, magazines, educational books, brochures, flyers, labels, cartons, and more. Digital printing covers security printing, variable data printing, short-run printing, and more.

Post-Press Services — finishing that completes the job

Finishing may include folding, cutting, drilling, creasing, binding, stitching, hardbound work, boxes, envelopes, and other job-specific work. Wherever possible we handle these stages in-house, so the finished piece arrives ready to use or dispatch. Our finishing services include cutting, folding, binding, laminating, Wiro binding, centre pinning, loop pinning, and more.

Packaging & Delivery

Printed material is packed for safe handling and dispatched to the agreed plan. Packaging is adapted to each order's format, quantity, destination, and handling needs.
