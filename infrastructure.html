Pre Press Machines

Ensuring accuracy of inputs to deliver great outputs

Pre-press is the preparation stage that helps each job reach the press with the correct files, settings, and specifications. Our designers and technical operators review incoming artwork, check print readiness, and prepare production files for accurate output.

We review artwork carefully and make necessary corrections before production so avoidable problems are caught early.

Our in House CTP (UVP 4648E) Model X with Gekco help us produce high quality plates rapidly and accurately. We also have a sophisticated in house Digital printer (Konica Minolta C 1060) for proofing.
